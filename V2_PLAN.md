# Ekonomiapp v2 roadmap

1. Transaction foundation: amount, currency, description, category, project, date.
2. Voice-first entry: Swedish natural-language commands for expenses and income.
3. Receipts/documents: upload first, then OCR and structured extraction.
4. Projects: Vardag, Hus, Bil, Resa, Företag, with room for more.
5. Money logic: recurring bills, committed/planned amounts, paid amounts, budgets and monthly views.
6. Currency: preserve original currency; support SEK and EUR without silently overwriting source values.
7. Backend: Cloudflare D1 for durable data, then authentication and sync across devices.
8. Intelligence: anomaly/attention prompts that explain unusual items without moralising.
9. Later: richer visual identity/themes and optional 3D house/project view.
